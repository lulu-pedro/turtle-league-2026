What players are saying

"Turtle League has created a truly special and rare environment that successfully balances fun competition with a culture of inclusion. I am so grateful for a community where we can challenge ourselves on the pitch while prioritizing safety and joy!"

Asya

"As a kid, I never played sports because as a fairly slow and uncoordinated person, I always felt that the best thing I could do for any team was just not be on it. Now at 49, my weekly soccer game is the best part of my week, and it would never have happened without PPS. PPS is a truly unique sports space where fun, community and learning are actually more important than winning, and people in their 20s to 70s play together."

Sarah E

"PPS is an essential community-centered organization. It's truly welcoming to all, regardless of age, ability, or gender. After a difficult day at the office, nothing cheers me up more than playing soccer with wonderful people who value camaraderie over competition."

Alexei E

"Turtle league has helped me find a home on the soccer field again after a decade of not playing. It is a necessary community to welcome underrepresented women, trans, and nonbinary players in a space that feels friendly and supportive to all levels. Turtle league is a truly special community and has transformed my experience in New York."

Dulany

"Turtle league is a safe, inclusive space that welcomes women, trans, and non-binary players of all different skill levels. When I returned to playing soccer as an adult, I was intimidated by the competitive energy of other leagues, but Turtle League prioritizes safe play and good vibes above all else. The result is a vibrant, tight-knit, diverse community that has spanned decades and always has room for one more. In a world where women and queer people are still regularly excluded from athletic spaces, Turtle League is truly special organization, on the pitch and off."

Angie G

"Turtle league is that rarest of New York institutions where amateur female athletes are taken seriously, where trans and non-binary folk are an integral part of the community, where players of all levels can be challenged without being intimidated, and where care and community are worth more than the final score. Here age truly ain't nothing but a number. For the love of the game, turtle league plays beautiful soccer with teams of beautiful people."

Emily W

"As possibly the oldest recreational soccer player in NYC, I'm 77, Turtle League has given me a place to play on a more competitive level with women. There are few all women's leagues in this city, usually with former collegiate players who are not open to an older player whose skill level was not formed as a child. We need more Turtle League!"

Sidney
