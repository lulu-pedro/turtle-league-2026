Turtle League

Our Mission

Turtle League is part of Prospect Park Soccer, a friendly, volunteer-run soccer collective. We're a mixed-level, community-first league for Women/Trans/Nonbinary players. We play competitive soccer where everyone belongs. Come for the soccer, stay for the people.

You'll find experienced and growing players on the same field. Some are advanced, some are coming back from injury, some just started playing in the last few years. We learn from each other, lift each other up, and leave the ego at the door. Our players all head to the bar after every game and celebrate together at the end of the season.

How to Play

We have four teams. We play one night a week, two games back-to-back, 40 minutes of 8v8 soccer with no keepers. The season is underway and rostered spots are full, but you can join as a free agent. Free agents sign up week-to-week on Meetup and fill in when we need players. Each free agent game costs $8. Click on the "Schedule & RSVP" to sign up to play.

Prospect Park Soccer

We're part of Prospect Park Soccer, a friendly, volunteer-run soccer collective where everyone gets to touch the ball and feel part of the game. We play inclusive, non-competitive soccer open to every skill level. And we make an extra effort to welcome beginners and players of all gender identities who have not always been given space on the field.

PPS is more than just soccer. It's also a social community where people connect, share laughs, and have fun together. Whether you're joining Turtle League or one of our other groups, you're part of a community that values inclusivity, respect, and good vibes.
