Turtle League

Our Mission

A mixed-level, community-first league for Women/Trans/Nonbinary players. We play competitive soccer where everyone belongs. Come for the soccer, stay for the people.

You'll find experienced and growing players on the same field. Some are advanced, some are coming back from injury, some just started playing in the last few years. We learn from each other, lift each other up, and leave the ego at the door. Our players all head to the bar after every game and celebrate together at the end of the season.

How to Play

We have four teams. We play one night a week, two games back-to-back, 40 minutes of 8v8 soccer with no keepers. The season is underway and rostered spots are full, but you can join as a free agent. Free agents sign up week-to-week on Meetup and fill in when we need players. Each free agent game costs $8. Click on the "Schedule & RSVP" to sign up to play.
