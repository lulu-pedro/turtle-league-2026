Before you sign up: Our House Rules

  1. This is a mixed-level league and there are players from various playing backgrounds. We are competitive but kind. This isn't footy league. Bring good energy and respect. Zero tolerance for meanness or aggressive behaviors. If you're spoken to more than three times about your behavior toward others, you will be asked to be on the sideline and possibly sit out from future games.
  2. Don't hurt each other physically or verbally. Be respectful to your teammates and your opponents. No slide tackles.
  3. No cleats, no exceptions. Must wear turf shoes or sneakers, nothing with spikes.
  4. Pause during the game for injuries.
  5. There are no official goalkeepers in this league. Captains are permitted to dedicate a central defender as a sweeper keeper type role. No hands.
  6. We self-officiate on the honor system. Captains make the difficult calls.

How to RSVP

Rostered Players (Signed up for the season) ▼
  1. RSVP on Luma on Thursday: Click the Luma link in your email or use the links below to confirm attendance.
  2. Team captains see your status: Captains will see on Luma who is going or not going.
  3. Last-minute drop: Update your status on Luma and notify your captain + team asap. Laura will see the change on Luma and manage the waitlist.
Free Agents (Play individual games) ▼
  1. Sign up on Sunday at 8pm on Meetup: Click the Meetup link to RSVP. To register for a game, you must be a member of Prospect Park Soccer on Meetup. If spots are available, you'll be added to the roster. If the game is full, you'll be placed on the waitlist.
  2. If you're marked as attending, you're in: You're filling in for a rostered player who is out.
  3. Tuesday PM payment: Watch for a Luma link to pay $8/game (emailed directly or posted in Meetup chat).
  4. Show up early: Arrive 5-10 minutes before game time to find out what team you're assigned to.

New to Prospect Park Soccer? Sign up on Meetup as a member. After your approval, you will be to RSVP on the Meetup Event.
