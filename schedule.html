House Rules

  1. This is a mixed-level league. We are competitive but kind. This isn't footy league. Bring good energy and respect. Zero tolerance for meanness or aggressive behaviors. If you're spoken to more than three times about your behavior toward others, you will be asked to be on the sideline and possibly sit out from future games.
  2. Don't hurt each other physically or verbally. Be respectful to your teammates and your opponents. No slide tackles.
  3. Pause for injuries.
  4. There are no official goalkeepers in this league. Captains are permitted to dedicate a central defender as a sweeper keeper type role. No hands.
  5. We self-officiate on the honor system. Captains make the difficult calls.

How to RSVP

Rostered Players ▼
  1. Opens Thursdays: RSVP window opens each Thursday on Luma
  2. RSVP on Luma: Click the Luma link for your game day and confirm attendance
  3. Tell your team: Message your captain and teammates to let them know you're playing
  4. Last-minute drop: Update your status on Luma and notify your captain + team. Laura will see the change and manage the waitlist
Free Agents ▼
  1. Sign up on Meetup: Click the Meetup link on Sunday and claim your spot
  2. Pay on Luma: After signing up on Meetup, you'll pay $8 per game on Luma
  3. Waitlist: If full, you're added to the waitlist on Meetup. Laura manages this
  4. Tuesday night: Laura proposes team assignments to captains for approval
  5. Game day: Find Laura when you arrive and she'll confirm your team assignment
  6. If running late: Message the general WhatsApp chat to let everyone know
