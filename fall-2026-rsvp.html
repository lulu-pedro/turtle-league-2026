Two ways to play

Team Player

Commit for the whole season on one of the 4 teams (8 players each, 32 spots total).
Team players are required to attend at least 4 games during the season.
RSVP on Luma when signups open to reserve your roster spot.

Free Agent

Prefer to play game-by-game?
Sign up as a free agent on Luma to fill in for rostered players who are out on a given week.
